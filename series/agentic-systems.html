About the series

{{ info.blurb }} The running question is when giving a system more control improves outcomes enough to justify the added cost and failure modes.

What you will learn

Research orientation

Compare each architectural change with a baseline. Ask what changed in the model, tools, context, search, compute, orchestration, or evaluation, and what the improvement cost. Use the literature reviews to examine the evidence and the labs to reproduce the course examples.

Running case study

AutoResearch starts with a hypothesis, an experiment, a metric, and a decision. Later blocks use the same case to reason about planning, compute budgets, experiment records, parallel work, and recovery.
